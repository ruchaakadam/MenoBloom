🌱 Everyday Wellness

💧 Stay adequately hydrated throughout the day.
🥗 Aim for a varied, nutrient-rich eating pattern rather than relying on a single food or supplement.
🚶‍♀️ Include regular physical activity that is appropriate for your abilities.
😴 Maintain a regular sleep and wake schedule when possible.
🧘‍♀️ Make time for relaxation, social connection or activities that help you manage stress.
🩺 Discuss persistent or concerning symptoms with a healthcare professional.
Important: Food and lifestyle suggestions cannot diagnose, prevent or treat a medical condition. Individual nutritional needs can differ, particularly if someone has other health conditions or takes medication. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for personalized advice.
