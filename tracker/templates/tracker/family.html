Welcome to HerBalance Family Support 💗

Menopause is a natural stage of life that can bring physical, emotional and lifestyle changes. Understanding these changes can help families provide better support, patience and care.

🌸 Understanding Her Changes

🔥

Hot Flashes

Sudden feelings of heat and sweating can occur. Providing a comfortable environment and understanding her discomfort can help.

😴

Sleep Problems

Hormonal changes can affect sleep. Encourage a calm bedtime routine and avoid dismissing tiredness as simply being lazy.

💭

Mood Changes

Irritability, anxiety or emotional changes may occur. Listening without judgement can make a meaningful difference.

🦴

Body Changes

Menopause can affect areas such as bone health, muscles, metabolism and overall energy.

🤝 How You Can Support Her

Small things can make a big difference

  • 👂 Listen to her concerns without immediately dismissing them.
  • 💬 Encourage open conversations about how she is feeling.
  • 🌿 Support healthy food, sleep and physical activity habits.
  • 🩺 Encourage regular health check-ups and discussions with healthcare professionals.
  • ❤️ Be patient when mood or energy levels change.
  • 🫶 Ask how you can help instead of assuming what she needs.

💬 What To Avoid

❌

"It's just in your head."

Her symptoms and feelings are real. Avoid dismissing her experiences.

❌

"Everyone goes through it."

Every woman's experience can be different. Give her space to describe what she is experiencing.

🩺 When should she talk to a healthcare professional?

If symptoms are persistent, severe, concerning or affecting daily life, encourage her to speak with a qualified healthcare professional. This website is intended to support awareness and tracking, not replace medical care.
