What is Menopause?

Understanding your body, one step at a time.

🌷 Menopause

Menopause is a natural stage of life when menstrual periods permanently stop because the ovaries gradually produce less of the hormones estrogen and progesterone.

What happens during menopause?

During the transition to menopause, hormone levels can change. These changes may affect periods, sleep, mood, energy levels and other parts of everyday wellbeing.

Common symptoms

🔥 Hot flashes
🌙 Sleep changes
💭 Mood changes
💧 Night sweats
❤️ Changes in wellbeing
🌸 Period changes
Remember: Every woman's menopause experience can be different. Tracking symptoms and noticing changes can help you understand your own wellbeing.
